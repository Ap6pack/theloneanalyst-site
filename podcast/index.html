The Lone Analyst Podcast

The internet's weird basement, live from a podcast booth. What happens when you read between the headlines.

The Analyst and Keiko Carrow take real headlines from AI research, cybersecurity, and tech policy and run them through a paranoia detector. Some episodes cover a supply-chain attack. Others follow a model that stopped showing its work. Real reporting, satirical theories, and a skeptic who checks the facts.
